\section{Next Week's Plan}
\label{sec:next_week}
%=======================================================================

In the upcoming week, research and development will focus on three key tasks:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{N\arabic*.},leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Algorithm Implementations \& New Metrics:} Deep-dive into algorithm details and formulations to refine implementations, eliminate reproducibility errors, and introduce metrics for drift adaptability.
  \item \textbf{Hyperparameter Benchmarking \& LODO Pipeline:} Study clustering hyperparameter behavior and prototype an experimental Leave-One-Dataset-Out (LODO) pipeline with relative scaling parameters on key algorithms.
  \item \textbf{Text Stream Clustering (\texttt{textclust}):} Explore the feasibility of incorporating \texttt{textclust} into the River-based benchmark framework if time permits.
\end{enumerate}
