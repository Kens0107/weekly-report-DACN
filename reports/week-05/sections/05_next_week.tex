\label{sec:next}

Two decisions from the mentors are needed before the runs start.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{D\arabic*.},leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Ceiling gate.} We chose $0.05$ after seeing Covertype. From most to least rigorous, it can be tied to batch $k$-means, made relative to each ceiling, or kept as a dated subjective choice.
  \item \textbf{Cost limit.} A recommended set needs a limit on how much slower than the default it may run, since P2R gains $0.008$ ARI on Covertype at 250 times the run time.
\end{enumerate}
